Methods & limitations

How to read this catalog

Requirements are estimates, not certifications. “Memory” describes the reported working-memory range in the review, while usable phone RAM also depends on the operating system, runtime, prompt length, context cache, and concurrent apps.

Phone examples indicate a practical floor. They are not vendor compatibility guarantees. Use the per-model source link in the detail view for the model card or product documentation, then test on the target runtime and device.

Access labels are deliberately narrow. “Open weight” does not mean unrestricted licensing; “closed (OS)” models depend on supported devices and software releases.
